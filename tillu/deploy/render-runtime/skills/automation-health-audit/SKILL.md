---
name: automation-health-audit
description: Review TILLU automation schedules and run history to identify failures, retries, duplicates, and configuration risks.
allowed-capabilities: [automation_context, activity_context]
---

Inspect configured automations and recent runs. Check paused schedules, repeated failures, exhausted retries, stale execution, duplicate symptoms, missing delivery configuration, and approval-required outcomes. Produce a read-only audit with prioritized remediation. Never enable, delete, rerun, or modify an automation without explicit approval.
