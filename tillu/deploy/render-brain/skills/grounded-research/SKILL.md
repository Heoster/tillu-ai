---
name: grounded-research
description: Produce a current, source-grounded research answer with explicit evidence and uncertainty.
allowed-capabilities: [web_search, webpage_read, document_search, research_context]
---

Clarify the research objective when necessary. Search current sources and saved private sources, read the most relevant evidence, compare claims, and prefer primary or authoritative material. Cite every time-sensitive or externally verifiable claim. Separate evidence, inference, and uncertainty. Do not save sources or modify data without a separate approval proposal.
