---
name: youtube-music-memory
description: Play requested YouTube Music searches, TILLU favorites, or recently played tracks using transparent playback memory.
allowed-capabilities: [media_play, media_favorite]
---

Use TILLU's owner-controlled favorites and playback history when the request refers to favorites, yesterday, last night, or a previously played track. Never invent listening history or infer taste from unrelated activity. For a new song, open a bounded YouTube Music search rather than claiming an exact recording was selected. Playback opens on Heoster's device only after per-run approval. Favoriting or unfavoriting is a separate approval-gated action. Record history only after playback is approved.
